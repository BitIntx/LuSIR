\begin{abstract}
This report summarizes LuSIR, a work-in-progress x4 super-resolution system trained
directly in vision space, without importing a pretrained text-to-image diffusion
backbone. The completed pipeline consists of a factor-4 VAE, deterministic
LR-to-HR-latent pretraining, conditional latent diffusion, and
condition-start fine-tuning on a 103k-image photo split, followed by a 133k
unique-image Stage 2 scale-up. The latest public XL-stage result uses a
500M-class full inference path: a wider Stage 2
condition encoder trained with a stronger degradation curriculum and a deeper
469.6M-parameter diffusion U-Net fine-tuned with decoded edge/highpass losses.
On the current \texttt{photo\_v3\_noise\_mix} sampled validation setup, the
Stage 4 XL edge-loss checkpoint improves over bicubic by +0.7195 dB PSNR.
Subsequent role-split and gated-residual probes show that bounded residual
parameterization can nearly preserve the Stage 2 condition output on mild
validation, reaching 25.0445 PSNR versus 25.0449 for condition-only, but it
still does not provide a net average gain. A direct residual diagnostic then
showed that the recoverable gap is dominated by high-frequency residuals, and a
small deterministic sparse-gate residual refiner improved mild val100 mean PSNR
from 25.0449 to 25.1178 with 86/100 wins over condition-only. A subsequent
detail-preserving curriculum adaptation produced the first sampled
gated-residual Stage 4 result to beat condition-only on both mean PSNR and a
clear majority of samples: 25.3406 PSNR and 71/100 wins versus 25.3103 for the
condition baseline. A decoded-detail residual refiner v2 then became the public
Colab default, reaching 25.6410 PSNR on the photo-detail validation mix. The
latest Stage 2 redesigns add multiscale context, balanced high-quality data,
and then a dual-context LSDIR scale-up. The completed dual-context run selects
step 98,000 for cleaner presets, with modest same-tool gains of 0.1362 dB on
\texttt{photo\_detail\_mix} and 0.1086 dB on \texttt{mild}; the final step
100,000 checkpoint is slightly safer on stronger degradation tails. A
subsequent image-space high-frequency detail branch v1b freezes that Stage 2
checkpoint and the Stage 1 decoder, selecting step 39,500 with +0.0461 dB PSNR,
+0.00268 SSIM, and 98/100 wins over the frozen base. This is the current detail
research candidate, but the visible change remains conservative and does not
solve the perceptual-detail gap.
\end{abstract}

\section{Objective}

The target task is single-image x4 restoration:
\[
128 \times 128 \rightarrow 512 \times 512.
\]
A later extension targets
\[
192 \times 192 \rightarrow 768 \times 768.
\]
The design goal is a practical latent diffusion SR model trained from LuSIR
checkpoints only. The system intentionally avoids Stable Diffusion, T2I
backbones, text conditioning, and imported generative priors from pretrained
diffusion models.

\section{Architecture}

The model is split into three trainable parts:
\[
\mathrm{HR\ image} \rightarrow \mathrm{VAE} \rightarrow \mathrm{HR\ latent},
\]
\[
\mathrm{LR\ image} \rightarrow \mathrm{condition\ encoder} \rightarrow
\mathrm{condition\ latent},
\]
\[
\mathrm{noisy\ HR\ latent}, \mathrm{condition\ latent}, t,
\mathrm{domain\ id} \rightarrow \mathrm{conditional\ U\mbox{-}Net}.
\]
The VAE decoder maps the denoised latent back to the 512px HR output. Domain IDs
are implemented for photo and anime/illustration data, although the completed
large-scale runs reported here use the photo split.

The stage numbers describe training order, not a mandatory serial inference
chain. The public Colab default is
\[
\mathrm{LR} \rightarrow \mathrm{Stage\ 2} \rightarrow
\mathrm{residual\ refiner} \rightarrow \mathrm{Stage\ 1\ decoder}.
\]
The current deterministic detail research candidate is
\[
\mathrm{LR} \rightarrow \mathrm{dual\mbox{-}context\ Stage\ 2}
\rightarrow \mathrm{Stage\ 1\ decoder} \rightarrow
\mathrm{detail\ branch\ v1b}.
\]
For generative comparison, the runtime path is
\[
\mathrm{LR} \rightarrow \mathrm{Stage\ 2} \rightarrow
\mathrm{Stage\ 3\ or\ Stage\ 4} \rightarrow \mathrm{Stage\ 1\ decoder}.
\]
Stage 4 is a Stage-3-derived replacement checkpoint, not a module applied after
Stage 3.

\section{Training Stages}

The project is organized as staged training rather than a single end-to-end run:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Stage 1 trains the factor-4 VAE on HR crops.
  \item Stage 2 trains a deterministic condition encoder to predict HR latents
        from degraded LR inputs.
  \item Stage 3 trains a conditional diffusion U-Net in latent space.
  \item Stage 4 fine-tunes diffusion from the condition latent at low timesteps.
  \item Stage 5 is planned few-step distillation. If implemented, it will
        replace the slower Stage 3/4 sampler rather than run after Stage 4.
  \item XL variants widen and deepen the condition encoder or diffusion U-Net
        while using partial checkpoint initialization where tensor shapes match.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Completed Implementation}

The repository currently includes:
\begin{itemize}
  \item manifest-based dataset loading with explicit domain IDs,
  \item on-the-fly x4 degradation pipelines,
  \item a factor-4 AutoencoderKL with 16 latent channels,
  \item deterministic LR-to-HR-latent pretraining,
  \item conditional latent diffusion with timestep and domain conditioning,
  \item DDIM/img2img sampling for validation,
  \item condition-start Stage 4 fine-tuning,
  \item condition-only validation tooling for isolating Stage 2 from diffusion,
  \item gated-residual \(\hat{x}_0\) prediction for bounded Stage 4 refinement,
  \item Stage 2 residual oracle diagnostics for low/high-frequency error
        isolation,
  \item a deterministic bounded residual refiner with sparse/open gate
        ablations,
  \item bf16 CUDA/ROCm training paths,
  \item W\&B logging, fixed validation samples, checkpoint evaluation, and
        recovery scripts for VM migration,
  \item partial checkpoint initialization for widened/deepened models,
  \item PyTorch DDP diffusion training with single-GPU fallback.
\end{itemize}

\section{Data and Degradation}

100-image validation split. The completed dual-context Stage 2 scale-up adds
30,000 unique LSDIR training images, for 133,450 unique training images and the

\section{Detail-Preserving Curriculum Adaptation}

A fixed-sample degradation audit confirmed that
\texttt{photo\_v3\_noise\_mix} overemphasized cleanup. On val100 it produced a
22.3599 bicubic PSNR baseline and 0.02040 mean LR chroma RMS error relative to
clean downsampling. The new \texttt{photo\_detail\_mix} increased the bicubic
baseline to 24.7357 and reduced chroma error to 0.00507.

The existing Stage 2 XL condition encoder already improved
\texttt{photo\_detail\_mix} to 25.3103 PSNR, so it remained frozen. The
teacher-supervised gated-residual Stage 4 was adapted for 12000 micro-steps
with batch size eight, gradient accumulation four, and learning rate
\(10^{-6}\).

The latest Stage 2 redesign preserves the selected XL predictor through partial
initialization, then adds a zero-output-initialized multiscale context branch.
Training exposure balances 100,000 COCO rows against 103,500 repeated
DIV2K/Flickr2K rows. The 55.50M-parameter model completed 50,000 micro-steps,
and step 46,000 was selected.

The selected model improves structure, color, and denoising but remains soft on
fine texture. A continuation using frozen ImageNet VGG16 features completed
12,000 micro-steps from step 46,000. Step 8000 had the best shortlist score,
26.0092, and improved decoded PSNR over initialization by 0.0101 to 0.0256 dB

the strongest noise mix by 0.0063 dB. Fixed contact sheets showed almost no
visible difference from initialization, and missing fine texture remained
smoothed. The continuation was therefore not promoted.

The completed scale-up changed capacity and data uniqueness together. The previous
HQ-balanced manifest contained 203,600 rows but only 103,550 unique images.
The new manifest adds 30,000 unique LSDIR training images, giving 133,450
unique training images plus the unchanged 100-image validation set. A second
zero-output-initialized multiscale context branch preserves the selected step
46,000 output before training and increases predictor capacity from 55.50M to
119.24M parameters.

A one-L40S smoke test reproduced the initial 24.48 dB decoded PSNR and 0.291
detail ratio. Batch 8 with gradient accumulation 4 used approximately
37.8/46.1GB VRAM at 99\% GPU utilization and approximately 0.75 micro-steps/s.
The completed long run targeted 100,000 micro-steps, or 25,000 optimizer
updates. Validation ran every 1,000 micro-steps, while ordinary milestones were
stored every 5,000 micro-steps to remain within disk budget.

The run completed all 100,000 micro-steps. Step 98,000 is the automatic
\texttt{eval/decoded\_psnr} best on \texttt{photo\_detail\_mix}; step 100,000
is slightly better on stronger degradations. Against selected step 46,000 under
the same comparison tool, step 98,000 improves \texttt{photo\_detail\_mix} by
0.1362 dB and \texttt{mild} by 0.1086 dB, while \texttt{photo\_v2} improves by
0.0540 dB and \texttt{photo\_v3\_noise\_mix} regresses by 0.0356 dB. The final
checkpoint changes the strong-preset deltas to +0.0668 dB and +0.0132 dB. This
is a modest reconstruction gain, not a solved perceptual-detail problem.

\section{High-Frequency Detail Branch v1}

The latest deterministic detail candidate is image-space, not another Stage 4
continuation. It runs
\[
\mathrm{LR} \rightarrow \mathrm{Stage\ 2\ dual\ condition}
\rightarrow \mathrm{Stage\ 1\ decoder} \rightarrow \mathrm{base\ SR},
\]
then applies a gated high-frequency detail branch using the base SR and
bicubic-upsampled LR image. Stage 2 and Stage 1 remain frozen, and Stage 3/4
diffusion sampling is not used.

The branch predicts a bounded RGB residual and a per-pixel gate. The residual
is projected through a local highpass operation before addition to the base SR,
limiting the model's route for global color or low-frequency structure changes.
The output convolution is zero-initialized, so step 0 exactly reproduces the
frozen Stage 2 + Stage 1 base output. The implementation is in
\texttt{tools/train/train\_detail\_branch.py},
\texttt{tools/eval/run\_fixed\_review\_detail\_branch.py}, and
\texttt{configs/detail\_branch\_v1b\_aug\_photo130k\_lsdir.yaml}.

A four-micro-step smoke test completed loading, evaluation, backpropagation,
one optimizer update, and checkpoint saving. Step 0 matched the base output
exactly at 24.6188 dB val100 PSNR; after one optimizer update the aggregate
PSNR delta was +0.00005 dB with 69/100 wins versus base. This is an integration
check only.

The first v1 long run was stopped at 7,800 micro-steps, or 0.234 epoch over the
133,450-image train manifest, because the early residual was safe but visually
too small. The v1b run kept the same model and loss, but added horizontal flip,
texture-biased crop retry, and weak HR color jitter while excluding rotation,
vertical flip, affine/perspective transforms, erasing, and mixup. It completed
40,000 micro-steps, equal to 10,000 optimizer updates with gradient accumulation
4, and selected step 39,500 by \texttt{eval/detail\_score}. At that checkpoint,
base PSNR is 24.6188, detail PSNR is 24.6649, the PSNR delta is +0.0461 dB,
base SSIM is 0.80013, detail SSIM is 0.80281, the SSIM delta is +0.00268, mean
PSNR delta is +0.0575, and wins versus base are 98/100. PSNR delta peaks at
step 38,500 (+0.0489 dB), SSIM delta peaks at step 37,000 (+0.00336), and final
step 40,000 reaches +0.0444 dB PSNR and +0.00277 SSIM with 98/100 wins. The
selected step 39,500 is therefore the current detail research candidate. It is
artifact-light and slightly sharper on texture-heavy crops, but still
conservative and below GT on fine surface detail. It is not yet the public
Colab default because the WebUI currently wraps the residual-refiner and
diffusion single-image runners.

\section{Limitations}

This is an engineering report for an active experiment, not a claim of a final
published method. The large-scale completed results are photo-domain only, while
anime/illustration domain conditioning remains implemented but not yet validated
at the same scale. The reported sampled PSNR values are useful for internal
checkpoint comparison, but they are not a full benchmark suite against external
SR methods. The detail-preserving curriculum improved sampled Stage 4 accuracy,
but the selected diffusion output remains conservative and does not recover all
visible fine detail. The latest deterministic detail branch improves the frozen
Stage 2 base slightly but remains conservative. The highest-signal next
directions are the \texttt{detail\_v1} fixed visual/perceptual review workflow,
single-image/tiled detail branch inference for Colab integration, a small
SSIM/MS-SSIM or gate-opening v1c ablation, and separation of the rare strong
degradation tail into a robustness curriculum or evaluation slice.
The completed VGG-feature continuation was not promoted into the public Colab
default because its small metric gains did not translate into visible detail
recovery.
